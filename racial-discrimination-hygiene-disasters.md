A customer reviewing his experience at **LE POSH NAILS** described repeated interactions that left him feeling unwelcome, raising concerns about customer service, workplace professionalism, and hygiene standards. According to the customer, the combination of perceived unequal treatment and unsanitary behavior ultimately convinced him not to return.

## Feeling Unwelcome

The customer explained that this was not his first visit but his **fourth** appointment.

He said that over multiple visits he noticed what he believed was a pattern in how he was greeted compared with other customers. According to his account, he did not receive the same warm welcome that he observed being extended to some other clients.

Instead, he said he was frequently met with a direct question such as:

> "What do you want?"

He described feeling as though he was being treated like an intruder rather than a paying customer.

## Hygiene Concerns

The customer also raised concerns about hygiene inside the salon.

According to the review, he observed a male technician picking his nose before touching a massage chair without washing his hands.

He described the incident as unacceptable and said it undermined his confidence in the cleanliness of the salon environment.

## Why First Impressions Matter

The customer emphasized that neither the front desk interaction nor the behavior of the technicians made him feel acknowledged or respected.

Regardless of intent, customers often judge a business based on greetings, body language, and visible hygiene practices. Small interactions can have a significant impact on whether clients feel comfortable returning.

## Editorial Perspective

Customer service begins the moment someone enters the business.

Every visitor should be greeted professionally and consistently. Differences in tone, attentiveness, or body language may be interpreted as unequal treatment, even when that is not the intention. Businesses benefit from training staff to provide courteous and consistent service to every customer.

Likewise, visible hygiene practices are fundamental in the beauty and wellness industry. Customers expect technicians to maintain clean hands, sanitize equipment, and follow professional standards throughout every appointment.

## Key Takeaways

- Every customer should receive a respectful and consistent welcome.
- Professional communication helps build trust.
- Proper hand hygiene is essential in beauty service environments.
- Visible sanitation practices influence customer confidence.
- Strong management and staff training help protect a salon's reputation.

## Final Thoughts

The experience described in this customer review illustrates how concerns about interpersonal treatment and hygiene can outweigh the quality of the services themselves. For salons, maintaining professionalism, consistent customer care, and strict sanitation standards remains essential to earning long-term trust.
